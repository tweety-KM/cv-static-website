01About

I started out behind a camera, not a keyboard.

A BA in Film & Television from the University of the Witwatersrand put me on set as a Second Camera Assistant — a job that runs on split-second problem solving, clear communication, and never missing a detail. Years of brand campaigns and in-store activations for names like Samsung and Diageo taught me the other half: how to read a room and sell an idea fast.

Somewhere in between, I got curious about what was running underneath everything — and started teaching myself to build it. I'm now AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner, comfortable across HTML, CSS, JavaScript and TypeScript, and putting together cloud-backed, AI-assisted tools for African contexts. Same eye for detail, new set.
