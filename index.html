01About

I started out behind a camera, not a keyboard.

An Honours in Film & Television from the University of the Witwatersrand put me on set as a Second Camera Assistant and Production Coordinator— a job that runs on split-second problem solving, clear communication and little to no room for missing a detail. Years of brand campaigns and in-store activations for names like Samsung, Mini Cooper, Lego and Diageo (J&B, Johnnie Walker) taught me the other half, whihc is the importance of how to read a room and sell an idea fast.

Somewhere in between, I got curious about what was running underneath everything and started teaching myself to build it. I'm now AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner, comfortable across HTML, CSS and growing in Python while also working towards an AWS Solutions Architect Associate Certification; putting together cloud-backed, AI-assisted tools for African contexts. Same eye for detail, new set.
